## 7. NON-NEGOTIABLE money/payment/receipt semantics
- An **invoice** records a request/claim (pending receivable), not proof of a payment. Its status tracks issued/partial/paid/overdue/void separately from fiscal status.
- A **Payment** is a distinct verifiable ledger event with amount minor units, currency, source, provider/bank reference, evidence, idempotency key, provider/bank authority, merchant ownership and allocations. Collected cash/revenue projection must be derived from verified payment events, not invoice face value or receipt count. “Paid invoice” remains an invoice with recorded allocations.
- An optional **Receipt** is a document acknowledging actual payment, issued only when merchant chooses to issue it or enables an approved setting to auto-send after verified gateway/virtual account payment. It can be generated later from an already paid invoice/payment, or manually for independently received payments after appropriate evidence. Receipt issuance is NEVER another incoming payment and must not duplicate receipts on replay.
- **Manual Mark Paid:** eligible only for invoices whose selected method is company **bank transfer**, with evidence/reconciliation and role-gated approval; **never** allow manually marking a Paystack/Flutterwave/Monnify/dynamic-account invoice paid. Provider redirects or user screenshots are never proof. Gateway/dynamic account status comes from authoritative provider verification and reconciliation.
- Partial 70/30 or 2–6 milestone schedules: one immutable issued invoice, exact deterministic minor-unit allocation, independent verified collections, due/stage states, no arbitrary duplicate claims. Refunds and reversals must preserve history and update balances without deleting money events.
- A quotation is an offer/estimate; it is not a demand for money and has no bank instructions or NRS fiscal invoice QR. Acceptance/conversion creates a **new reviewed invoice draft** with tax source reevaluation and linked source quotation.
- Credit/debit notes adjust records with original-document linkage, review and applicable NRS/tax treatment; never erase issued invoices.
- Reports/taxes should distinguish billed amount, outstanding receivables, verified collections, net after refunds, estimated tax liability, official filing and actual remittance. Do not derive formal revenue recognition or statutory tax simply from invoice status.
- See `docs/project/INVOICE_RECEIPT_PAYMENT_AUTHORITY.md`, `PAYMENT_AND_RECEIPT_PHASE0H.md`, `NUMBERING_AND_CREDIT_AUTHORITY.md`, `COMPLIANCE_NRS_NDPA.md`.

## 8. NRS compliance and OWASP controls
- **OWASP**, not OSWAP. Baseline is ASVS 5.0-informed / ASVS Level 2-appropriate: deny-by-default server-side object/tenant RBAC+ABAC, high-risk owner/staff MFA and step-up, hardened OIDC/OTP/reset/session/cookies, CSRF/Origin, CSP/HSTS, PII minimization, input validation, secure file uploads, immutable tamper-evident finance/security audit trail, idempotent provider webhooks, tested replay/refunds, rate limit, backup and incident response, NDA/NDPA compliance. A passing CodeQL scan is not full ASVS compliance.
- Never copy insecure Finzefy donor Express OTP, permissive uploads, credential logs, Sequelize `sync({alter:true})`, wallet API or simulated auth into secure backend.
- Nigeria Revenue Service e-invoice: structured fiscal JSON/XML with official IRN, CSID, submission status, validation QR and provider authority; a PDF, portal/payment QR or demo link is NOT by itself official NRS e-invoice. Current legal scope, VAT categories, due dates, WHT, exemptions and NRS API must be validated against official sources and expert review before activation. Distinguish tax filed from tax paid. NDA/NDPA data restrictions and mandated financial retention override simple erasure preference.
- No automatic delete of issued finance documents after 7/14 days. Soft-delete archive can hide ordinary draft UI records temporarily; immutable financial/audit history must be preserved under the applicable retention rules.
