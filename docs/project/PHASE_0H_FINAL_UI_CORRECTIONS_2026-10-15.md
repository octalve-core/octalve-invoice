The nine screenshots supplied on 15 October 2026 cover the prior More page, PalmPay five-tab navigation and profile layout, premium settings page, subscription lifecycle states, delete confirmation sheet, invoice template customization and reporting dashboard. They are visual *reference patterns* only. Do not use PalmPay/third-party branding, personal data, payment account details or their sample claims in Octalve UI.

1. **Mobile navigation:** five reachable bottom tabs are Home, Invoices, Receipts, Clients, Me. "Me" replaces the former miscellaneous More destination and provides direct premium settings with avatar and separate support/settings links. Existing Other Services remains independently reachable through dashboard navigation and its route; mobile nav is not six tabs. Icons, colour, active states, keyboard focus and iOS safe-area are handled in `BottomNavbar`.
2. **Premium Settings:** redesigned settings as linked account profile plus three clean sections: Account & billing, General settings, Preferences & support. Routes to subscription, reports, profile, invoice, business, theme, FAQ, recoverable deleted records.
3. **Subscription:** removed fictitious completed history, pseudo payment methods and fake payment successes. Added plans/monthly/quarterly/yearly options with a clearly non-billable review summary, explanatory provider/tax disclosures. Proposed NGN model: Free ₦0 with 25 invoices/month; Starter ₦3,600 with 250; Growth ₦7,700 with 1,000; Business ₦11,800 with proposed higher limits. Quarter 5% and annual 15% discounts are PROPOSALS. Plan features requiring backend are labelled planned; no subscription change or payment capture. This is *pricing research*, subject to product owner signoff, not active SKU entitlements.
4. **Deletion:** shared RecordDirectory opens a second destructive confirmation dialog/bottom sheet with mandatory reason. After confirmation in the PREVIEW session it hides the item from the active list but keeps it in `/dashboard/settings/deleted` where it is visible with reference/type/title/reason/time and can be restored. 14-day **proposed** recovery window is informative only—no timer or purge runs in Preview. **Finance records, issued invoices/receipts and audit history are never hard-deleted at day 14**. Future production policy must require tenant authorization, owner/admin protection and immutable event logs; staff must not be able to conceal payments by archive. Reports still use original immutable sample event projection, not this display filter.
5. **FAQs:** new searchable accordion page with non-promissory answers on payments, receipts, accounts, customization, record retention, costs and support.
6. **Themes:** new responsive invoice appearance editor with Modern/Classic choice, curated safe fonts, accent hex, comfortable/compact, header style, logo position, footer and presentation watermark preview. Context is ephemeral. Selected Modern browser/PNG invoice uses supported CSS variables; Classic Ledger browser and native PDF receive accent colour. The approved Sapphire template remains the real renderer, and **Modern native PDF theme styles / custom font uploads remain unqualified**. Don't silently promise exact native PDF parity with arbitrary client-selected fonts before proper font embedding and golden tests.
7. **Reports:** existing `/dashboard/report` now uses full dashboard content width, responsive overview cards, time filter, Recharts invoice/collections time series and status donut, top invoiced clients and genuine report options. Data are explicitly illustrative and assembled from existing Preview invoices and independently verified sample payment entries. No receipts are counted as additional revenue. Growth indicators and historical saved reports are not fabricated. Exports remain disabled until verified server ledger.

### Unit economics/gating
- No per-invoice transaction fee is recommended for manual bank-transfer invoices. A payment event—not issuance of an unpaid invoice—is revenue/collections authority.
- Provider processing charges (Paystack, Flutterwave, dynamic account) need signed commercial terms; merchant billing markup and statutory VAT need legal and provider verification. Do not hardcode unverified payment fees.
- Confirm subscription entitlements, overages, free tier sustainability, support/service costs and migration eligibility during approved backend architecture. Proposed caps are UI and product strategy only.
- The free limit of 25/month is deliberately more useful than 7/month, but is substantially lower than Zoho's published Free annual invoice cap. Product positioning/value proposition should not claim lowest-price or feature parity.
