# NIGERIA COMPLIANCE DESIGN
Nigeria Revenue Service e-invoice integration: verify current official Merchant Buyer Solution docs before implementation. Separate human-readable invoice number from NRS-compliant fiscal component and IRN. Use official classification and tax resource mapping; do not hardcode universal VAT. Model invoice validation, issuance/fiscalization, signed/confirmed reporting where appropriate to approved integration mode, QR data, IRN, UBL JSON/XML mapping, submission record, retries/outbox and PARTIAL payment-state updates where documented. Tax calculations are exact and shared with invoice PDFs. NRS tax reporting != actual tax remittance; never claim automated remittance absent verified authorized payment channel.
Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and NDPC guidance: minimize PII, document data map/purpose/controller roles, restrict cross-merchant access, protect signature assets, establish retention/erasure subject to legal financial retention obligations, export/DSR process, breach response, audit evidence and subprocessors. Preserve original historical issued documents lawfully and immutably.
