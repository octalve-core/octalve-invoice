## Authority and dimensions
A merchant's original invoice is a billed claim; an actual collection is a distinct verified money event; a receipt acknowledges that same event and adds **zero** new cash; neither collection nor billing automatically proves earned revenue. A supplier bill/expense incurred is not necessarily a settled disbursement. Store all amounts as signed bounded **integer minor units** (NGN kobo) or exact decimal arithmetic at domain boundaries; reject NaN, overflow, negative invalid inputs and mismatched currency. Do not use JS floating-point for authoritative balances.

Every real KPI requires: `merchantId`, reporting basis and status, currency, period start/end and timezone, effective date, source IDs, role permission, correction revision, FX policy if multiple currencies, and calculation-version. Tenant-scoped event IDs must deduplicate before aggregation. Events cannot be created by displayed UI records. Keep one reusable aggregation contract for mobile, desktop, exports and APIs.

### Proposed metric dictionary (each is pending semantic approval)
| Metric / user-facing label | Qualified source, formula and exclusions | Effective date / corrections |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Issued invoice value** | Sum original issued snapshot payable amounts within period and currency. Exclude drafts, quotations, voided-unissued documents and duplicates; show corrections separately, not by rewriting original face. | Official issuance timestamp; correction note has independent time. |
| **Outstanding receivables** | Eligible original issued claims + validated opening receivable + authorised debit adjustments - effective credit allocations - verified payment allocations + formally reversed applications, bounded by account policy. Do not aggregate across currencies without approved FX. | As-of date; adjustment effective time auditable. |
| **Gross verified collections** | Sum distinct final confirmed incoming money events, by actual receipt/value date and currency, including separately classified customer advances/loans if cash received. Never add Invoice/Receipt totals to it. | Verified bank/provider/approved manual bank-clearing evidence date; annotate late reports. |
| **Collections allocated to invoices** | Sum unique applied payment allocations against issued/open eligible invoices. Cannot exceed supported available funds or eligible invoice balance unless stored unapplied separately. | Allocation posting date; separate payment date retained. |
| **Unapplied cash / customer advance** | Confirmed incoming funds minus total valid allocations, refunds and other classified applications, with tenant/customer attribution. Not automatically recognised revenue. | Money date and subsequent application events separately. |
| **Net money collected** | Gross confirmed incoming minus actually settled outgoing customer refunds, chargebacks and payment reversals in the selected period. Show processor fees as distinct cost / settlement reconciliation; do not silently reduce invoice principal by fees. | Money movement's actual date. Prior-period revisions require adjustment policy, not silent editing. |
| **Revenue recognised** | Only confirmed eligible performance/delivery events posted under owner-approved cash/accrual/accounting policy and tax rule. **BLOCKED until basis and timing confirmed.** Customer deposit and financing are not automatic revenue. | Service performance/satisfaction date or approved cash-basis equivalent; immutable event revision. |
| **Supplier bills/payables** | Validated supplier obligations and opening payables less allocated verified disbursements and authorised credits. Draft/approved/pending statuses distinct. | Bill recognition and separate settlement date. |
| **Expense incurred** | Approved cost recognition by chosen basis/classification; distinguish COGS, OPEX, capex/asset, refundable advance, and owner drawings. **Do not sum all bank withdrawals as expense.** | Cost-incurred date, separately journaled corrections if accounting enabled. |
| **Actual spending / settled outflow** | Distinct completed bank/cash disbursement events classified as operating cost, asset, owner drawing, tax, refund, debt settlement, etc. Exclude mere payment instructions, overdue bills or failed transfers. | Confirmed value date; chargebacks/recoveries separate. |
| **Available customer credit** | Total granted/earned transferable credit less idempotently applied-to-invoice amounts and settled refunds, considering pending refund reservations so value cannot be spent twice. | Credit issuance, reservation, application and settlement independently timed. |
| **Credits applied** | Valid credit allocations only; never treat credit note face value and application as two reductions. | Allocation effective time. |
| **Refund requested / reserved / settled** | Three separate numbers and lifecycle states. Requested is not money out. Settled is verified outbound cash; reservations affect credit availability. | Respective request, approval/reservation and external settlement dates. |
| **Gross margin / net profit** | Recognised revenue less matching verified COGS; net profit also needs approved OPEX, other income/expense, depreciation and tax conventions. **BLOCKED** until record completeness/basis approved. | Reporting period and approved cost allocation; disclose excluded categories. |
| **Cash flow (net verified movements)** | Distinct incoming completed monetary events less actual completed outgoing monetary events. Show funding/financing/owner contributions separately from operating revenue; exclude invoices and receipts as duplicate events. | Bank/clearing value date, reversals as distinct auditable postings. |
| **Receivables ageing / DSO / working capital** | As-of open balances grouped by due date; DSO requires definition of credit sales/average eligible A/R and period length; working capital requires complete qualifying current asset/liability scope. **Do not manufacture from partial sample data.** | As-of end date with chosen timezone and original due dates. |

**Display conventions.** A zero denominator shows “Not available”, not 0% or Infinity; specify round-half-up/bankers policy before backend. Percent change uses `(current - comparison)/abs(comparison) × 100` only when previous nonzero, plus sign and direction labels. Show “Example data” while authentic tenant records are absent. FX: native-currency amounts first; conversion disabled unless source/provider, quote timestamp, rate side, precision and revaluation policy are documented. Business reports must not merge currencies by concatenating numeric sums.

## Worked non-authoritative test fixtures
1. Issued ₦150,000; confirmed ₦100,000 allocated; open ₦50,000; optional ₦100,000 receipt: **gross cash +₦100,000 once**, receipt cash +₦0, billed claim ₦150,000. Earned revenue **unknown** without accounting policy.
2. ₦200,000 supplier electricity bill approved today but paid next month: period A shows eligible payable ₦200,000, settled disbursement ₦0; period B shows confirmed outflow ₦200,000 and payable decrease, not a second expense without documented basis.
3. ₦200,000 issued invoice: provider confirms ₦120,000; a separate, authorised merchant bank receipt confirms ₦80,000; balance ₦0. Never change provider event status or bypass its verification. Two incoming money events, one invoice, optional receipts.
4. ₦50,000 customer credit: reserve ₦20,000 for authorised refund, allocate ₦30,000 to future invoice. Available after reservations ₦0; refund only counts as settled cash when actual payout verifies.
5. Same provider event delivered five times: stable provider merchant+reference+event identity and matching amount/currency produce **one** verified money event, one allocation; no duplicate receipts.
6. A ₦100,000 deposit for unperformed work increases cash by ₦100,000 but earned revenue remains unasserted; label as customer advance/contract liability subject to approved policy.

## Authorization, acceptance and decisions
- All queries and materialized metrics deny cross-tenant reads/writes at service + persistence, enforce role visibility and audit who calculated/exported what. Display a separate status for omitted/unverified source records.
- Property tests for amount conservation, commutative idempotent webhook replay, mixed channels, overpayment/unapplied, refunds, reversals, fees, credit allocation and reserve, timezone cutover, FX disabled, large integer bounds, zero/negative denominators and immutable snapshots.
- **OWNER TO DECIDE**: first accounting level (L1 financial management; L2 advanced bills/credits; L3 full double-entry separately approved), cash/accrual/mixed basis, performance recognition date, tax/personal-use classifications, reporting completeness/rounding rules, statement adjustments, cross-currency/FX policy, and profit disclosure threshold.
- Until approved, **do not label collections “revenue”, mark a simulated P&L as real, or treat 20 what-if calculators as ledger output**.
