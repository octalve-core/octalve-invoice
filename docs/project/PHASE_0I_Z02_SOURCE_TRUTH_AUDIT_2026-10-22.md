## Compliance and financial safety
- Invoice number format remains OL-00001INV (company initials + five digits + document suffix) and the existing numbering authority remains unchanged.
- Unreviewed standard/zero-rated/exempt/outside-scope/conditional treatments are **not tax determinations**. Tax Centre permits editing only a proposed merchant reference, proposed date and jurisdiction; changing these reverts policy status to research-pending. A select control never marks a classification legally reviewed.
- Invoice=amount requested/pending; receipt=verified recorded payment truth. No paid action from unverified gateway/dynamic account payments; no real receipts created or communicated.
- New catalogues and drafts are temporary in-memory preview state. Refreshing can reset inputs; that is a Phase 1 persistence limitation, not a claim of operational data safety.
- User-visible desktop pop-ups/mobile sheets stay scrollable with invisible rails; existing `#0064E0`, fixed `#000A16` sidebar, responsive cards and five invoice steps remain.
- Unlinked cash, original-issued snapshots, NRS fiscal responses and real customer records must not be fabricated.
