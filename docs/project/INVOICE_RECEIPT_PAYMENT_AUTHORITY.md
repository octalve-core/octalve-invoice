# Invoice → Payment → Optional Receipt Contract — Owner Authority, 15 October 2026
## Essential distinction
1. An **invoice** is a claim or request for payment for goods/services. It exists separately from how or when the merchant receives money. Its status can subsequently become partially paid, paid, overdue, refunded/cancelled, etc. Paying an invoice does **not** erase the invoice.
2. A **payment** is a separate financial event with idempotency key, amount, currency, date, source, method, evidence, verification and linked allocation(s). Posting money to one or more invoices requires validated merchant ownership and reconciliation. No client-side "mark paid" operation is authoritative.
3. A **receipt** acknowledges receipt of payment and is an **optional explicitly issued document**. Neither invoice creation, payment detection, marking an invoice paid, nor quotation acceptance auto-issues a receipt. Merchants choose whether to issue it. Consent/choice should be explicit in a secure backend workflow. Do not create a duplicate receipt when replaying a payment webhook or repeated UI action.
4. A **manual receipt** is possible when a merchant received payment for an invoice issued outside Octalve Invoice or an independent cash/bank transaction. Record a separate verified/manual-payment entry, then allow explicitly issuing a receipt. Manual mode must not mean invented or provider-verified payment. Clearly identify manual/unverified evidence and role-gated approval rules.
5. A receipt can be **linked** to an existing issued invoice/payment allocation or **standalone** tied to an independently recorded payment. Both get their own receipt number, and the invoice number remains unchanged.
6. **Refund** is a separate traceable money movement with status and audit history. A refunded receipt is not deleted, and an associated invoice is not silently restored or cancelled. Any eligible credit note/reference to original invoice, tax adjustments and credit-balance ledger operations are separately governed.
7. **Quotations** are estimates/proposals. Sent/accepted/declined/draft status must never be treated as payment confirmation or silently turn into invoice/receipt. Conversion requires an explicit user action and new document allocation.
## Backend architecture and anti-double-counting constraints (Phase 1+)
- Separate models: Invoice, Payment, PaymentAllocation, Receipt, ReceiptPaymentLink, ManualPaymentEvidence, Refund, CreditNote, CreditAllocation. Use merchant-scoped UUID identifiers; visible sequences are immutable, server allocated by merchant and type. Constraints for cross-tenant foreign keys.
- Payment status is reconciled from trustworthy evidence, with idempotent verified webhooks and maker/checker for manual verification above policy thresholds; no fake gateway connectivity.
- Receipt creation is a separate explicit state transition; configurable opt-in can be proposed later but no automatic issuance in the approved scope. Add audit trail of issuer, timestamp, source payment(s), version and immutable generated document snapshots.
- In cash-flow reporting, count each verified payment once, not once as Invoice + once as Receipt + once as Payment Received. Invoices report receivables; receipts evidence actual payments. Link events instead of summing document totals.
- An external payment may cover multiple invoices, and an invoice may be settled via multiple partial payments; a single receipt can acknowledge a supported payment amount without breaking allocations. Validate currency/tax/refunds consistently.
- RBAC, MFA for sensitive actions, audit logging, transactional reference allocation, CSRF/rate limits, anti-forgery document links and rigorous test coverage before any production deployment.
## Phase 0G preview fixture contract
- `getPreviewRecords` creates read-only sample lists; it does not issue invoices, receipts, or payments.
- An example receipt carries `relatedInvoiceId` and `paymentOrigin: "linked-invoice"`; another example is `paymentOrigin: "manual"` with no invoice link. All are **illustrative**, not verification of funds.
- Sample invoices can be paid without any receipt sample. Quotation statuses are non-financial. Actions for send, refund, delete and other financial mutations display accurate unavailable notices and never alter ledger data.

## Revised owner direction — 15 October 2026 evening
The merchant may select automatic receipt handling for **verified online payments** in Invoice Settings: OFF (default), GENERATE, GENERATE_AND_EMAIL. This supersedes blanket prohibition on automatically issued receipts; the default remains manual/optional and auto-issuance is a guarded opt-in. It is impossible to auto-issue until the server has durably verified and applied the payment allocation. A payment that was already recorded and paid remains selectable to request a receipt later, with anti-duplication and authorization. Emails must be queued/retried idempotently with delivery evidence, not assumed delivered.
An ordinary bank transfer to the merchant's independent bank account does not provide webhook access. The merchant can **record a bank-cleared payment manually only** on an eligible manual-bank-transfer invoice, with amount, reference, date, clear evidence and authorised review. The UI must never claim this is provider-verified. An invoice committed to Paystack, Flutterwave or Monnify dynamic account cannot be manually marked paid by changing a global payment dropdown; it requires provider status verification, signature+reference+amount+currency and matching merchant connection. Transfer exceptions require a controlled separate adjustment/dispute workflow rather than arbitrary mark-paid override.
Differentiate **issued/billed invoices**, **outstanding receivables**, **verified collections**, **net collected after verified refunds**, and **receipt document counts**. Billing an invoice is not itself proof cash has arrived and is not always earned revenue for formal accounting/tax purposes. Receipt documents NEVER contribute additional cash flow. Refunds do not silently delete receipts, void documents or restore invoice debts; reversal/allocation rules must be explicit. Fiscalization/payment-status syncing to NRS occurs only after official API validation.
